Oct 12, 1492 – 1504·Event

The Voyages of Columbus

On October 12, 1492, Christopher Columbus, a Genoese sailor seeking a western sea route to Asia on behalf of Spain, landed on an island in the Bahamas that its Lucayan inhabitants called Guanahani. His four voyages did not reach Asia, but they began permanent contact between Europe and the Americas, setting off the Columbian Exchange, Spanish conquest and colonization, and a catastrophe for the Taíno and other Indigenous peoples of the Caribbean.
