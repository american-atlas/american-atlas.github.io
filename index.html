Oct 12, 1492 – 1504·Event

The Voyages of Columbus

On October 12, 1492, Christopher Columbus, a Genoese sailor sailing for Spain in search of a western route to Asia, came ashore on an island in the Bahamas that its Lucayan people called Guanahani. He never reached Asia. His four voyages between 1492 and 1504 began lasting contact between Europe and the Americas, and set off the Columbian Exchange and the Spanish conquest. They also brought catastrophe to the Taíno and other peoples of the Caribbean: enslavement, forced labor, and epidemics destroyed much of the population within a few decades. Columbus is still honored by some Americans and condemned by others, and the old language of “discovery” describes only one side of the encounter.
