Module 1 of 5 · 1450–1774

Roots of Rebellion & the Imperial Crisis

Indigenous confederacies, imperial war debt, and the road to colonial resistance

0% of module

Long before Britain taxed colonial paper and tea, the Haudenosaunee Confederacy practiced council governance in North America. Britain’s victory in the French and Indian War (1754–1763) redrew the continent’s map, left London deeply in debt, and sparked a fierce constitutional dispute over parliamentary taxation, street clashes in Boston, and intercolonial union at Philadelphia.

Guiding Question

Why did Britain’s triumph in 1763 unravel its North American empire within a single decade?
