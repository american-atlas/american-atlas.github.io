2e:T44f,Foreign policy was his passion. With his national security adviser, Henry Kissinger, he played the Communist rivals against each other. After China invited an American table tennis team in 1971, Kissinger held secret talks, and in February 1972 Nixon made a widely televised visit to Beijing. Later in 1972 he became the first president to visit Moscow, where he and Leonid Brezhnev signed the Strategic Arms Limitation Treaty and the Anti-Ballistic Missile Treaty. He withdrew American troops from Vietnam gradually under “Vietnamization,” and the Paris Peace Accords of January 1973 ended the participation of U.S. ground forces. At home, his administration imposed more new regulation than any since the New Deal: he signed the law creating the Occupational Safety and Health Administration, proposed the Environmental Protection Agency, and signed the Clean Air Act amendments of 1970, the Endangered Species Act, and the Safe Drinking Water Act.
